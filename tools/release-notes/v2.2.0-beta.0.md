Ponko is an offline-capable AI app for Android: chat (image, file and document input) +
drawing + tagging, each of which can be served over its own OpenAI-compatible API.
All inference runs on-device: apart from the optional in-app LoRA download, no feature
needs the network. No telemetry; your data stays on your phone.
See the [README](https://github.com/visitor257/Ponko#readme).

**This is a pre-release.** It installs alongside the stable app (package
`com.litertchat.app.beta`).

## What changed since 2.1.3

**stable-diffusion.cpp is upgraded to upstream master (2026-11-13)** - 79 upstream commits,
ggml included.

That brings **MiniMax-H3-Image** and **P-H3-Image** (#2324, #2272). H3 video was already
there; what was missing was the Sol-H3 VAE decode path (#2317). CHROMA also gets its own
version id (#2237) instead of borrowing another model's.

Fixes in there that reach features which already worked: hires now honours the step count
you ask for (#2227), diffusers-named SDXL is recognised without the ComfyUI prefix (#2247),
LoRA scalars stay F32 and are merged as F32 when needed (#2263, #2302), and Krea2 Kohya LoRA
names no longer vanish (#2301).

- **New family `MiniMax-H3-Image / P-H3-Image`**: diffusion model + Qwen3-VL encoder +
  Sol-H3 VAE. Reference-image editing works from the same checkpoint
- **New `Text connector` slot**, needed when pairing the 4B encoder. Appended at the end of
  the slot table rather than next to `llm`, for the same reason the tokenizer slot was

## Verified on a device

512×512, 6 steps, CFG 1.0, Euler + Discrete, shift left empty, on a 15.6 GB phone. 651
tensors loaded, the image comes out clean, no engine warnings.

- `minimax_h3_image_pruned-Q2_K.gguf` - 7.2 GB
- `p-h3-image_text_encoder_int8_convrot.safetensors` - 4.8 GB
- `minimax_h3_image_vae_fp16.safetensors` - 1.1 GB

## Please read before reporting

**Expect about 25 minutes for one 512×512 image.** 13.4 GB of weights on a 15.6 GB device
means the diffusion steps are read from storage rather than computed - the log shows
available memory falling to 1.2 GB. Slow, not broken.

Two things that waste time if you get them wrong:

- **Use the P-H3-Image text encoder, not the 32B one.** 4.8 GB instead of 17 GB, and it
  handles both text-to-image and reference-image editing
- **Use the MiniMax-H3-Image VAE.** The MiniMax-H3 *video* VAE and the P-H3-Image VAE are
  both incompatible and fail at decode

More steps do not help either; the model is trained on a six-step schedule.

三者都能各自开一个 OpenAI 兼容 API。所有推理都在设备本地完成——除了可选的 App 内 LoRA

### 相对 2.1.3 改了什么

**stable-diffusion.cpp 升级到上游 2026-11-13 的 master**——79 个上游提交，ggml 一并跟上。

由此带来 **MiniMax-H3-Image** 和 **P-H3-Image**（#2324、#2272）。H3 视频本来就有，
缺的是 Sol-H3 VAE 的解码路径（#2317）。`CHROMA` 也终于有了自己的版本 id（#2237），
不再借用别人的。

其中对已有功能有实质影响的修复：hires 现在遵守你申请的步数（#2227）、
diffusers 命名的 SDXL 不带 ComfyUI 前缀也能识别（#2247）、
LoRA 标量保持 F32 并在需要时按 F32 合并（#2263、#2302）、
Krea2 的 Kohya LoRA 名字不再丢失（#2301）。

- **新家族 `MiniMax-H3-Image / P-H3-Image`**：扩散模型 + Qwen3-VL 编码器 + Sol-H3 VAE。
  同一个 checkpoint 也支持参考图编辑
- **新增 `Text connector` 槽位**，配 4B 编码器时需要。追加在槽位表末尾而不是放在 `llm`
  旁边，理由和当初的 tokenizer 槽位一样

### 报告问题前请看

**一张 512×512 预计要 25 分钟。** 13.4 GB 权重放在 15.6 GB 内存的设备上，扩散的每一步
都是从存储里读数而不是在算——日志里可用内存掉到 1.2 GB。是慢，不是坏。

两个搞错就白等的地方：

- **文本编码器用 P-H3-Image 那个，不要用 32B 的。** 4.8 GB 对 17 GB，且文生图和参考图
  编辑都能用
- **VAE 必须用 MiniMax-H3-Image 的。** MiniMax-H3 的**视频** VAE 和 P-H3-Image 的 VAE
  都不兼容，会在解码阶段失败

加步数也没用——这个模型是按六步调度的。
